## Project 13: Social Media Tourism

### Description:
An aviation company that provides domestic as well as international trips to the customers now wants to apply a targeted approach instead of reaching out to each of the customers. Hence they have collaborated with a social networking platform, so they can learn the digital and social behavior of the customers and provide the digital advertisement on the user page of the targeted customers who have a high propensity to take up the product.

### Skills & Tools Covered:
1. Discriminant Analysis
2. Logistic Regression
3. Python
4. Exploratory Data Analysis
5. KNN
6. My Learnings

### Method Used: 
For the given classification problem, mathematics, statistics, various machine learning algorithms and prediction techniques have been applied to build multiple models like logistic regression, naive bayes, k-nearest neighbors, random forest, etc to predict the customers propensity of buying tickets based on their behaviour and activities on social networking platforms. Then the best and final model is opted for prediction on data and based on the results from all the reports, various business insights and recommendations were provided to the company.
